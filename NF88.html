Dying to be Me

Book Name Dying to be Me
Author/Publications Anita Moorjani
Language English
Genre Non-Fiction
Label NF88
Description Anita Moorjani recounts her experience of a serious illness and a near-death episode, which she interprets as a turning point in how she understands fear, self-acceptance and healing. The memoir combines personal narrative with spiritual reflections on living authentically. Readers may approach its accounts as the author’s own interpretation of her experience.
