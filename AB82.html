The Alchemist

Book Name The Alchemist
Author/Publications Paulo Coelho
Language English
Genre Fiction
Label AB82
Description Paulo Coelho’s philosophical novel follows Santiago, a young Andalusian shepherd who travels in search of a treasure near the Egyptian pyramids. Along the way, encounters with mentors and setbacks teach him to listen to his heart and recognise opportunities. The story reflects on purpose, courage, fate and pursuing one’s dreams.
