Description Agatha Christie’s Hercule Poirot mystery centres on a wealthy heiress found murdered aboard the Blue Train travelling through Europe. A valuable jewel and a complicated set of relationships provide clues and motives. Poirot examines the passengers’ accounts and hidden tensions to uncover the truth behind the crime.
