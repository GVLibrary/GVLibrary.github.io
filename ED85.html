Organizational Behavior

Book Name Organizational Behavior
Author/Publications Stephan Robbins, Timothy Judge, Neharika Vohra
Language English
Genre Education
Label ED85
Description This textbook examines how individuals and groups behave within organisations and how organisational structures influence performance. Topics commonly include motivation, leadership, communication, teams, conflict, culture and organisational change. It combines research and workplace examples to help students and managers understand behaviour and improve decision-making and collaboration.
