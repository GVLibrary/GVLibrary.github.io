Description Rudyard Kipling’s stories include the adventures of Mowgli, a boy raised by wolves in the Indian jungle, as well as tales about other animals. The stories explore belonging, law, courage and the balance between different communities. Adaptations vary, but the collection is known for its memorable animal characters and vivid setting.
