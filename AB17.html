Life is What You Make it

Book Name Life is What You Make it
Author/Publications Preeti Shenoy
Language English
Genre English Fiction
Label AB17
Description Preeti Shenoy’s novel follows Ankita, a young woman whose promising life takes an unexpected turn as she struggles with mental illness and recovery. It explores ambition, relationships, family expectations and the importance of understanding mental health, presenting a story of vulnerability, resilience and hope.
