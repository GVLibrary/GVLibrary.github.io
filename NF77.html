Description Dale Carnegie’s classic self-help book offers practical principles for communicating effectively, building relationships and influencing others without hostility. It emphasises listening, showing genuine interest, acknowledging others and handling disagreement tactfully. Its examples aim to help readers improve personal relationships, leadership and everyday professional interactions.
