Anna Karenina

Book Name Anna Karenina
Author/Publications Leo Tolstoy
Language English
Genre English Fiction
Label AB21
Description Leo Tolstoy’s classic novel explores love, marriage, family, social convention and personal freedom in nineteenth-century Russian society. Its intertwined stories follow Anna’s passionate affair and the contrasting domestic and spiritual struggles of other characters, including Levin. The book examines the costs of living against society’s expectations.
