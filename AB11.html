Papillon

Book Name Papillon
Author/Publications Henri Charriere
Language English
Genre English Fiction
Label AB11
Description Henri Charrière’s memoir recounts his imprisonment in a French penal colony and his repeated attempts to escape. It describes brutal conditions, unlikely friendships, determination and the longing for freedom. Blending adventure with a portrait of the penal system, the book centres on resilience in the face of confinement.
