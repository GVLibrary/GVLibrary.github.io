Book Name The Namesake
Author/Publications Jhumpa Lahiri
Language English
Genre English Fiction
Label AB64
Description Jhumpa Lahiri’s novel follows Gogol Ganguli, the American-born son of Bengali immigrants, as he negotiates his name, family heritage and personal identity. Moving between the United States and India, the story explores generational differences, belonging, love and the complex experience of living between cultures.
