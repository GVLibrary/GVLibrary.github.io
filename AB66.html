Description Arthur Conan Doyle’s collection features Sherlock Holmes and Dr Watson solving baffling cases through observation, deduction and careful reasoning. Each story presents a different puzzle involving crime, deception or unusual circumstances. Holmes’s analytical methods and Watson’s accessible narration helped establish many conventions of modern detective fiction.
