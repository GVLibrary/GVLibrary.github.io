A History of Economic Thoughts

Book Name A History of Economic Thoughts
Author/Publications Eric Roll
Language English
Genre Non-Fiction
Label N99
Description Eric Roll surveys the development of economic ideas, tracing major schools and thinkers from early political economy through later theories. The book places economic arguments in their historical and intellectual contexts, showing how ideas about value, production, markets and distribution evolved. It is intended as a structured introduction to the history of economic thought.
