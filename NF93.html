Book Name The Made in India Manager
Author/Publications R. Gopalakrishnan, Ranjan Banerjee
Language English
Genre Non Fiction, self help
Label NF93
Description R. Gopalakrishnan and Ranjan Banerjee discuss management and leadership through the lens of Indian organisational culture. The book considers how local values, social context and business realities influence the way managers lead people and make decisions. It offers reflections for readers seeking to develop practical, context-sensitive leadership skills.
