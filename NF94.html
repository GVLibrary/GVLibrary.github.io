What Got You Here Won't Get You There

Book Name What Got You Here Won't Get You There
Author/Publications Marshall Goldsmith
Language English
Genre Non Fiction, self help
Label NF94
Description Marshall Goldsmith examines how behaviours that help people succeed early in their careers can become obstacles at higher levels of leadership. Through practical examples, he identifies habits such as interrupting, taking credit and resisting feedback, and suggests ways to change them. The book focuses on self-awareness, feedback and sustained professional improvement.
