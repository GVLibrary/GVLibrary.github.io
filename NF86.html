Bhagwad Gita as it Is

Book Name Bhagwad Gita as it Is
Author/Publications Swami Prabhapada
Language English
Genre Non-Fiction, Spirituality
Label NF86
Description A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada’s edition presents the Bhagavad Gita with translation and commentary from the Gaudiya Vaishnava tradition. It explains the dialogue between Krishna and Arjuna in terms of devotion, duty, the self and spiritual liberation. The commentary reflects a specific religious interpretation of this Hindu scripture.
