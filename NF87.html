We Should All be Feminists

Book Name We Should All be Feminists
Author/Publications Chimamanda Adichi
Language English
Genre Non-Fiction
Label NF87
Description Chimamanda Ngozi Adichie makes an accessible case for feminism by drawing on personal experience and everyday examples of gender bias. She argues for a society in which people are not limited by gender stereotypes and in which equality benefits everyone. The short book is often used to prompt discussion about gender and culture.
