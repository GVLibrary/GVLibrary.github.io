Book Name Fool Me Once
Author/Publications Harlan Coben
Language English
Genre Fiction
Label AB91
Description Harlan Coben’s thriller follows Maya, a former military pilot who sees what appears to be her supposedly dead husband on a home security recording. Her investigation raises unsettling questions about her family and past. As clues accumulate, the story builds suspense around trust, memory, secrets and the difference between appearance and truth.
