The Naked Ape

Book Name The Naked Ape
Author/Publications Desmond Morris
Language English
Genre Non-Fiction, science
Label NF85
Description Desmond Morris examines human behaviour from an evolutionary and zoological perspective, considering social life, reproduction, parenting and group behaviour. Written for general readers, it asks how humans resemble other animals and how biology may influence culture. Some interpretations are debated, so it is best read as a popular-science argument.
