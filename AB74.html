The Blue Umbrella

Book Name The Blue Umbrella
Author/Publications Ruskin Bond
Language English
Genre Fiction, children, Indian
Label AB74
Description Ruskin Bond’s children’s novella follows Binya, a village girl who acquires a beautiful blue umbrella that attracts everyone’s attention. The shopkeeper Ram Bharosa becomes jealous and tries to obtain it. A gentle story of envy, generosity and forgiveness, it evokes the landscapes and everyday life of the Himalayan hills.
