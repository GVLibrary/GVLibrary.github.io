Tuesdays with Morrie

Book Name Tuesdays with Morrie
Author/Publications Mitch Albom
Language English
Genre English Fiction
Label AB50
Description Mitch Albom recounts his visits with Morrie Schwartz, his former university professor, who is dying from ALS. Their Tuesday conversations explore love, work, family, ageing, forgiveness and death. The book is a reflective memoir about what gives life meaning and how human connection can reshape priorities.
