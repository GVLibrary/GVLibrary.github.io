Jane Eyre

Book Name Jane Eyre
Author/Publications Charlotte Bronte
Language English
Genre Fiction, classic
Label AB77
Description Charlotte Brontë’s classic novel follows an orphaned girl who grows into an independent young woman and becomes a governess at Thornfield Hall. Her love for Mr Rochester is challenged by a disturbing secret. The book explores social class, morality, gender, self-respect and the search for emotional and personal independence.
