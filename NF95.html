Moonwalking with Einstein

Book Name Moonwalking with Einstein
Author/Publications Joshua Foer
Language English
Genre Non Fiction, self help
Label NF95
Description Joshua Foer’s book explores the science and history of memory through his attempt to train for the United States Memory Championship. He investigates techniques used by memory competitors and considers how attention, practice and mnemonic systems improve recall. The book combines reportage, personal experiment and accessible discussion of human cognition.
