Description Daphne du Maurier’s Gothic novel follows a young woman who marries the wealthy widower Maxim de Winter and moves to his imposing estate, Manderley. The lingering presence of his first wife, Rebecca, unsettles the new marriage. Suspense gradually reveals secrets about memory, jealousy, identity and appearances.
