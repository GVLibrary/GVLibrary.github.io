Strengths Finder 2.0

Book Name Strengths Finder 2.0
Author/Publications Tom Rath
Language English
Genre Non Fiction, self help
Label NF92
Description Tom Rath’s workplace-development book encourages readers to identify and build on their strongest talents rather than concentrating only on weaknesses. It introduces a strengths assessment and explains how personal talents can be developed into productive habits. The book is intended to support self-awareness, career development and more effective teamwork.
