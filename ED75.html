Description This textbook introduces key features and challenges of the Indian economy, including growth, agriculture, industry, services, poverty, employment, public finance and economic policy. It explains how institutions and government decisions affect development. The precise emphasis varies by author and edition, but the book is intended to support study of India’s economic structure and performance.
