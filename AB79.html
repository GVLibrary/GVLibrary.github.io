Description This volume brings together two Charles Dickens classics. A Tale of Two Cities depicts love, sacrifice and upheaval during the French Revolution, while Oliver Twist follows an orphan navigating poverty, exploitation and crime in Victorian London. Together, the novels examine injustice, social inequality, compassion and redemption.
