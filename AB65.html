Kane and Abel

Book Name Kane and Abel
Author/Publications Jeffery Archer
Language English
Genre English Fiction
Label AB65
Description Jeffrey Archer’s novel traces the parallel lives of William Lowell Kane, born into American privilege, and Abel Rosnovski, a Polish immigrant who rises from hardship. Their rivalry develops across decades as ambition and circumstance bring them into conflict. The story examines power, wealth, family and the lasting consequences of revenge.
