Words that Sell

Book Name Words that Sell
Author/Publications Richard Bayan
Language English
Genre Education
Label ED10
Description Richard Bayan’s reference guide collects persuasive words and phrases for advertising, sales copy and promotional writing. It helps writers find more vivid or specific ways to describe products, benefits and experiences, with vocabulary grouped by purpose or subject. The book is a practical resource for copywriters, marketers and anyone crafting persuasive messages.
