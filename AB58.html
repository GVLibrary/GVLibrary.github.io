My Siter, The Serial Killer

Book Name My Siter, The Serial Killer
Author/Publications Oyinkan Braithwaite
Language English
Genre English Fiction
Label AB58
Description Oyinkan Braithwaite’s darkly comic thriller follows Korede, a practical nurse whose beautiful younger sister Ayoola repeatedly kills her boyfriends. Korede helps conceal the crimes, until her feelings for a colleague complicate her loyalty. The novel explores sibling bonds, beauty, violence and the moral limits of protecting family.
