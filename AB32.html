Book Name Theodore Boone: The Abduction
Author/Publications John Grisham
Language English
Genre English Fiction
Label AB32
Description John Grisham’s young-adult legal thriller follows aspiring teenage lawyer Theodore Boone when his friend April disappears. Familiar with courtrooms and criminal law, Theo tries to help uncover what happened while facing risks beyond his experience. The story combines a missing-person mystery with courtroom knowledge and adolescent courage.
