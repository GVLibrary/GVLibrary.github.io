Book Name Treasure Island
Author/Publications Robert Louis Stevenson
Language English
Genre Children
Label CH79
Description Robert Louis Stevenson’s adventure novel follows young Jim Hawkins, who discovers a map leading to buried pirate treasure. His voyage brings him into conflict with the charismatic but treacherous Long John Silver and a crew of mutineers. The story of danger, loyalty and courage helped define the modern pirate-adventure genre.
