Description Paramahansa Yogananda describes his spiritual development, teachers and experiences, and his efforts to introduce yoga and meditation to readers beyond India. The book discusses self-realisation and spiritual practice through personal narrative and accounts of remarkable encounters. It is a widely read work of twentieth-century spiritual autobiography.
