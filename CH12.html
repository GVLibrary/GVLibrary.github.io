Frog and Toad are Friends

Book Name Frog and Toad are Friends
Author/Publications Arnold Lobel
Language English
Genre Children
Label CH12
Description Arnold Lobel’s classic early-reader collection follows the gentle adventures of two friends, Frog and Toad. Short, humorous episodes show them helping one another, facing small worries and enjoying ordinary days. Simple language and warm illustrations make the book an enduring introduction to reading and to themes of friendship, patience and kindness.
