Amelia Bedelia

Book Name Amelia Bedelia
Author/Publications Peggy Parish
Language English
Genre Children
Label CH19
Description Peggy Parish’s humorous early-reader book introduces Amelia Bedelia, a well-meaning housekeeper who interprets instructions literally. Her misunderstandings lead to comic mishaps, especially when familiar expressions are taken at face value. The story uses wordplay to entertain young readers while illustrating the differences between literal and figurative language.
