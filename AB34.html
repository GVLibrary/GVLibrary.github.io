Book Name The Phantom Tollbooth
Author/Publications Norton Juster
Language English
Genre English Fiction
Label AB34
Description Norton Juster’s imaginative children’s classic follows Milo, a bored boy who travels through a mysterious tollbooth into a fantastical kingdom. Wordplay, puzzles and eccentric characters turn the journey into an adventure about language, mathematics, curiosity and the rewards of paying attention to the world.
