Marketing 4.0 - Moving from Traditional to Digital

Book Name Marketing 4.0 - Moving from Traditional to Digital
Author/Publications Philip Kotler
Language English
Genre Education
Label ED05
Description Philip Kotler and co-authors discuss how marketing changes as consumers move between online and offline environments. The book examines connected customers, digital engagement, brand advocacy and the integration of traditional marketing with digital channels. It offers a framework for understanding customer journeys and adapting marketing strategy to a connected world.
