Wings of Fire - An Autobiography

Book Name Wings of Fire - An Autobiography
Author/Publications A P J Abdul Kalam
Language English
Genre Non Fiction
Label NF91
Description A. P. J. Abdul Kalam’s autobiography recounts his childhood, education and career in Indian aerospace and missile development, including his work with scientists and engineers. It reflects on perseverance, teamwork, scientific ambition and public service, offering an account of how personal discipline and collaborative effort contributed to his achievements.
