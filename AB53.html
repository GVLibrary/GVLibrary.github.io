Girl in white cotton

Book Name Girl in white cotton
Author/Publications Avni Doshi
Language English
Genre English Fiction
Label AB53
Description Avni Doshi’s novel examines a strained relationship between a daughter and her mother, whose memory is deteriorating. Set against family history and social expectations in India, it explores resentment, care, unreliable memory and the complicated bonds between women. Its intimate narrative questions how much we can truly know another person.
