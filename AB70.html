The Immortals of Meluha

Book Name The Immortals of Meluha
Author/Publications Amish
Language English
Genre English Fiction
Label AB70
Description Amish’s mythological fantasy reimagines the story of Shiva, a tribal leader invited to the prosperous kingdom of Meluha. As he encounters political conflict, social divisions and a looming threat, he is drawn into a destiny that may transform him into a revered figure. The novel blends Indian mythology with adventure and speculative history.
